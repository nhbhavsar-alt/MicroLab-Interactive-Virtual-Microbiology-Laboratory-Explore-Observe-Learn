Activity 3 — Gram-stain interpretation

Choose the observed colour and read the interpretation. Colour alone does not identify a bacterial species.

Select an outcome to view its interpretation.

Four key steps

  1. Crystal violet — primary stain
  2. Gram’s iodine — mordant
  3. Alcohol or acetone-alcohol — decolourizer
  4. Safranin — counterstain

Over-decolourization, culture age and smear thickness can affect the result. Use appropriate controls and your laboratory SOP.
